Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications

I am currently an Assistant Professor in the School of Artificial Intelligence at Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications (BUPT). Before joining BUPT, I was a postdoctoral researcher at the Institute of Trustworthy Networks and Systems, Tsinghua University, led by Prof. Yunhao Liu. I received my Ph.D. from Tsinghua University in 2024 under the supervision of Prof. Zheng Yang, and my B.E. from BUPT in 2019.
